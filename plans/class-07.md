# Class 7 — My family is crazy! 🤪
## Teacher's Guide / Lesson Plan

### Objectives
By the end of this class, the student can:
- Name family members (husband, sister, brother, daughter, son, grandchildren)
- Describe people's appearance ("She is tall", "He is young")
- Describe personality ("She is funny", "He is crazy")
- Use frequency adverbs ("She always...", "He never...")
- Gossip about family/friends in English (casual, fun tone)
- Combine descriptions: "My sister is tall and funny"

### Class Structure (60 minutes)

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 0-5 min | Warm-up review | "How are you feeling today?" (Class 6 review). Then: "Tell me about your family — who do you live with?" |
| 5-15 min | Vocabulary | 9 new words. Focus on adjectives — they're FUN because she can describe real people she knows. |
| 15-20 min | Describing people drill | Show photos (celebrities, cartoon characters) — she says one sentence: "She is [adj]" or "He is [adj]." Build to two: "She is tall AND funny." |
| 20-30 min | Dialogue practice — 1 & 2 | Role-play: she is Cleci gossiping with Marina. Encourage expressive delivery — gossiping should be FUN! |
| 30-40 min | Speaking: Describe YOUR family | She describes her actual family members. You ask: "Is she tall?" "Is he funny?" Build descriptions together. |
| 40-50 min | Dialogue practice — 3 & 4 | Focus on "always" and "never" — they add drama! Ask: "Who in your family ALWAYS does something funny?" |
| 50-55 min | BBC 6-Minute English | Show the podcast together. Listen to 2 minutes of one episode. Talk about what she understood. |
| 55-60 min | Wrap-up | "Describe one person in your family — three sentences!" Celebration! |

### Speaking Practice Ideas
- Family photo description: she shows photos, describes each person
- "Gossip game": you describe someone (real or fictional), she guesses who
- "Always/Never": rapid-fire — "Who always cooks?" "Who never exercises?"
- Celebrity game: show a picture, she gives 2-3 adjectives
- Build sentences progressively: "Marina" → "Marina is funny" → "Marina is funny and tall" → "Marina always talks!"

### Common Errors to Watch
- Word order: "She is tall" not "She is alta/tall is she"
- "He is" vs "She is" — PT speakers sometimes confuse because "ele/ela" sounds similar
- "Always" placement: "She always cooks" not "She cooks always"
- Adjective agreement: no gender! "He is beautiful / She is beautiful" (same word)
- "My sister have" → "My sister has" (third person -s, though keep it light)
- "Crazy" — reassure her it's playful/affectionate in this context, not mean

### Self-Study Tool: BBC 6-Minute English Podcast
- Show her how to find it: YouTube channel "BBC Learning English" → playlist "6 Minute English"
- It's FREE, short (6 minutes), and has subtitles
- Pick ONE episode together (choose a fun topic: family, food, travel)
- Goal: listen to it 3 times this week (1st = just listen, 2nd = with subtitles, 3rd = try to understand without subtitles)
- Reassure: she won't understand everything! That's FINE. Even catching 3-4 words is progress.

### Key Phrases to Drill
- "My [family member] is [adjective]."
- "She is tall and beautiful."
- "He always [verb]!" / "She never [verb]!"
- "He is so funny!" / "She is so crazy!"
- "My husband is..." / "My sister is..."
- "Can you believe...?" / "Really?!"

### Carry-Forward Vocabulary (use in ALL future classes)
- Family members: husband, sister, brother, daughter, son
- Adjectives: tall, short, funny, crazy, beautiful, young, old
- Adverbs: always, never
- These adjectives combine with ANY future topic (describing places, food, etc.)
- "Always" and "never" make any sentence more expressive

### Progress Check (after Class 7)
Student should now be able to:
- ✅ Everything from Classes 1-6
- ✅ Name family members in English
- ✅ Describe people's appearance and personality
- ✅ Use "always" and "never" for habits
- ✅ Have a simple gossiping conversation about people she knows
- ✅ Combine multiple adjectives: "She is tall and funny"
- ✅ Use BBC podcast as a self-study resource
