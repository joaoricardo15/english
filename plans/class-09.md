# Class 9 — Let's go for a trip! ✈️
## Teacher's Guide / Lesson Plan

### Objectives
By the end of this class, the student can:
- Use "I'm going to + verb" for future plans
- Name travel essentials (trip, hotel, beach, airport, bag, ticket, passport)
- Ask "Where is...?" for directions
- Communicate at a hotel ("I have a reservation", "Two nights, please")
- Use time expressions for plans (tomorrow, next week, this weekend)
- Combine travel with all previously learned vocabulary

### Class Structure (60 minutes)

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 0-5 min | Warm-up review | "What did you do on Saturday? Tell me using: First... Then... Finally..." (Class 8 review) |
| 5-15 min | Vocabulary | 9 new words. Star of the class: "I'm going to + verb" — it maps directly to Portuguese "vou + infinitivo" so it should feel natural! |
| 15-20 min | "Going to" drill | Rapid-fire: you give a verb, she makes a sentence. "Pack" → "I'm going to pack." "Travel" → "I'm going to travel." |
| 20-30 min | Dialogue practice — 1 & 2 | Role-play: she is Cleci planning and packing. Encourage excitement about the trip! |
| 30-40 min | Speaking: Plan HER dream trip | "Where are you going to go?" "When?" "What are you going to do?" Build a full trip plan together. |
| 40-50 min | Dialogue practice — 3 & 4 | Focus on hotel check-in. Ask: "Have you ever stayed at a hotel in another country? What did you say?" |
| 50-55 min | Forvo demonstration | Show Forvo.com/app. Search for 5 words she finds hard to pronounce. Listen to real people. Practice together. |
| 55-60 min | Wrap-up | Full trip narration: "I'm going to travel! First, I pack. Then, I go to the airport..." |

### Speaking Practice Ideas
- Dream trip planning: she plans a full trip (where/when/what/who with)
- Hotel role-play: full check-in sequence (you are the receptionist)
- Airport simulation: "Where is gate 5?" "Where is the bus stop?"
- "I'm going to" chain: each person adds one thing to the plan
- Compare: "What are you going to do this weekend?" — real plans!

### Common Errors to Watch
- "I go to travel" → "I'm going to travel" (need full structure)
- "I made a travel" → "I took a trip" (travel is verb, trip is noun)
- "Beach" pronunciation — /biːtʃ/ not /bɪtʃ/ (the long "ee" is critical!)
- "The hotel" stress — ho-TEL (second syllable), not OH-tel
- "Where is..." without rising intonation — drill the question melody
- Forgetting "a": "I need taxi" → "I need a taxi"

### Self-Study Tool: Forvo (Pronunciation)
- Show Forvo.com (website) and/or app
- It has REAL people from many countries pronouncing words
- Search for 5 travel words together: hotel, airport, ticket, beach, passport
- Listen to American vs British pronunciation
- She can use it anytime she's unsure how to say a word
- Bonus: search for her own name and see how English speakers say it!

### Key Phrases to Drill
- "I'm going to travel next week."
- "Where is the hotel?"
- "I have a reservation."
- "Two nights, please."
- "I'm going to pack my bag."
- "Where is gate 5?"
- "How much is the ticket?"

### Carry-Forward Vocabulary (use in Class 10)
- Trip, hotel, beach, airport, bag, ticket, passport
- "I'm going to + verb" — this is her future tense going forward
- "Where is...?" — essential question for any situation
- Time expressions: tomorrow, next week, this weekend

### Progress Check (after Class 9)
Student should now be able to:
- ✅ Everything from Classes 1-8
- ✅ Talk about future plans ("I'm going to...")
- ✅ Name travel vocabulary (airport, hotel, ticket, etc.)
- ✅ Ask "Where is...?" for locations
- ✅ Check into a hotel (basic conversation)
- ✅ Use Forvo to check pronunciation independently
