# Class 3 — I love this show! 📺
## Teacher's Guide / Lesson Plan

### Objectives
By the end of this class, the student can:
- Express likes and dislikes about TV shows and movies
- Use "I love", "I like", "I don't like" with simple nouns
- Use basic opinion adjectives (funny, sad, good, boring)
- Agree with someone using "Me too!"
- Distinguish between "show" (series) and "movie" (film)

### Class Structure (60 minutes)

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 0-5 min | Warm-up | Ask in PT: "What did you watch this week?" Get her talking about her shows |
| 5-15 min | Vocabulary review | Go through the 9 new words. Ask her to pronounce each one. Correct gently. |
| 15-25 min | Dialogue practice — Dialogues 1 & 2 | Read aloud together. She reads one character, you read the other. Switch roles. |
| 25-30 min | Speaking: Personalization | Ask HER opinions: "Do you like funny shows?" "What show do you love?" She answers with target phrases. |
| 30-40 min | Dialogue practice — Dialogues 3 & 4 | Same pattern. Focus on "I don't like" — it's harder for beginners (the contraction). |
| 40-50 min | Free conversation | Have a real conversation about shows. Use only target language. Help her form sentences. |
| 50-55 min | Netflix trick demo | Show her how to change audio/subtitles on Netflix. Do it live on the call. |
| 55-60 min | Wrap-up | Review 3 key phrases: "I love it!", "I don't like it", "Me too!" Encouragement. |

### Speaking Practice Ideas
- "What do you watch at night?" → She answers with "I watch..."
- "Is it funny or sad?" → She practices adjectives
- "Do you like [show name]?" → She uses "I like / I don't like"
- "Me too!" / "Really?" — practice natural reactions
- Ask about SPECIFIC shows she watches (use real titles from her Netflix)

### Common Errors to Watch
- "I no like" instead of "I don't like" (very common for PT speakers)
- Pronouncing "movie" as "moovee" (should be closer to "moo-vee")
- Confusing "funny" (engraçado) with "fun" (divertido) — both are OK at this level
- Forgetting the contraction in "It's good" (saying "It good")

### Self-Study Tool: Netflix Subtitle Trick
- Show her live how to change: Settings → Audio → English, Subtitles → Portuguese
- Pick a show she's ALREADY watched (she knows the story)
- Rule: maximum 15 minutes, no pausing, no stress
- It's NOT studying — it's watching TV

### Key Phrases to Drill (repeat these many times)
- "I love this show!"
- "I don't like sad movies."
- "It's really good!"
- "Me too!"
- "Is it funny?"

### Carry-Forward Vocabulary (use in ALL future classes)
- I love, I don't like, show, movie, funny, sad, good, boring, me too
- Core pattern: "I [like/love/don't like] + [adjective] + [noun]"
- Question pattern: "Is it [adjective]?" / "Do you like [noun]?"
