# Class 10 — I can do this! 🎉
## Teacher's Guide / Lesson Plan

### Objectives
By the end of this class, the student can:
- Use "I can + verb" to express ability and confidence
- Use survival phrases ("Can you repeat?", "I don't understand", "More slowly, please")
- Review ALL vocabulary from classes 1-9 through a celebratory story
- Feel proud of her achievements (explicit confidence building)
- Have a clear plan for continuing English independently
- End the course feeling this is a BEGINNING, not an end

### Before Class (send Monday)
Message template:
> Hi [name]! 🎉 This is our LAST class! Open Class 10 and:
> 1. Listen to ALL vocabulary — these are your "survival phrases"!
> 2. Read ALL 4 dialogues — the story is about YOU!
> 3. Think about: what was the BEST thing you learned in 10 weeks?
> See you in class — let's celebrate! 🥳

### Class Structure (60 minutes)

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 0-5 min | Warm-up review | "Tell me about a trip you want to take!" (Class 9 review). Celebrate: "Your English is really good now!" |
| 5-15 min | "I can" statements | Go through ALL 10 "I can" statements together. She reads each one aloud. Celebrate each one! |
| 15-20 min | Survival phrases drill | These are her "emergency kit" — practice until automatic: "Can you repeat?", "More slowly, please" |
| 20-30 min | Grand review role-play | One continuous scene through ALL class themes: greeting → routine → restaurant → doctor → family → chores → travel. She does it all! |
| 30-40 min | Dialogue practice — 3 & 4 | Focus on the celebration and forward-looking messages. Ask: "What are you going to practice this week?" |
| 40-50 min | Self-study plan | Together, write her personal "Coffee English" plan on paper. 7 days, 10 min each. What will she do each day? |
| 50-55 min | Certificate moment | Read the "I can" list one final time. Say: "Congratulations! You can speak English!" |
| 55-60 min | Farewell | Emotional closing. Remind her: "This is just the beginning!" Agree on monthly check-in plan. |

### Speaking Practice Ideas
- "10 Classes in 10 Sentences": one sentence per class theme, she says all 10 in a row
- Grand role-play: continuous scene visiting all 10 themes (5 min uninterrupted)
- "I can" checklist: she reads each statement and demonstrates with an example
- "What surprised you?" discussion: what was easier/harder than expected?
- Future planning: "What are you going to do next week for English?"

### Common Errors to Watch
- "I can to speak" → "I can speak" (no "to" after "can")
- Low confidence: she may say "I can't..." — redirect to what she CAN do
- "Repeat again" → "Can you repeat, please?" (more natural)
- This class is about CELEBRATION, not correction. Minimize error correction today!
- If she makes errors, smile and move on — today is about confidence

### Self-Study Tool: Study Plan for Continuing Alone
The "Coffee English" Plan — 10 minutes with morning coffee:
- **Monday:** 1 BBC Learning English video (YouTube)
- **Tuesday:** Review 5 words from course app
- **Wednesday:** Describe her day aloud (alone, to the mirror)
- **Thursday:** Netflix with English subtitles
- **Friday:** 3 sentences about her week (voice message to teacher)
- **Saturday:** 1 English song, read lyrics
- **Sunday:** Rest or repeat favorite

Three Rules:
1. Never more than 15 minutes
2. Never miss two days in a row
3. Use English for things she already enjoys

### Key Phrases to Drill
- "I can speak English!"
- "Can you repeat, please?"
- "I don't understand."
- "More slowly, please."
- "How do you say ___?"
- "Congratulations!"
- "I did it!"
- "See you soon!"

### Carry-Forward (for independent study)
- "I can" pattern for expressing ability
- Survival phrases (repeat, understand, slowly) for ANY English interaction
- The self-study plan as her ongoing roadmap
- Monthly check-ins with teacher (agree on format: WhatsApp voice message?)

### Final Achievement Checklist
By the end of this course, the student can:
- ✅ Greet someone and make small talk
- ✅ Describe her daily routine
- ✅ Express likes, dislikes, and opinions
- ✅ Go shopping and ask prices
- ✅ Order food at a restaurant
- ✅ Describe symptoms and visit a doctor
- ✅ Describe family and people
- ✅ Talk about chores in sequence
- ✅ Make future plans for travel
- ✅ Ask for help when she doesn't understand
- ✅ Continue learning INDEPENDENTLY

### Emotional Notes for the Teacher
- This class should feel like a CELEBRATION, not a test
- Prepare something personal: a note, a message, a "certificate" (even informal)
- Let her talk more than you — it's HER moment
- If she cries (common with adult learners who surprise themselves), let her
- End with: "This is not goodbye. This is 'see you soon!'"
- Agree on a monthly check-in (15 min voice call or WhatsApp exchange)
