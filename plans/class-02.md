# Class 2 — What do you do? (Lesson Plan)

## Objectives
- Student can describe her daily routine using present simple ("I wake up", "I cook", "I ride my bike")
- Student can use time phrases: "in the morning / in the afternoon / at night"
- Student can answer "What do you do every day?"
- Student learns Google Translate voice feature as a pronunciation tool

## Class Structure (60 min)

### 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Greet in English: "Good morning! How are you?"
- Quick Class 1 review: "What do you like for breakfast?" (expect: "I like coffee / bread / fruit")
- Bridge to Class 2: "And after breakfast, what do you do?"

### 2. Vocabulary Review (10 min)
- Go through the 10 new words — say each, she repeats
- Tricky sounds: "wake" (weɪk), "ride" (raɪd), "cook" (kʊk — short vowel, not "cooke")
- Drill time phrases as a set: "in the morning / in the afternoon / at night"
- Quick association game: you say a time of day, she says an activity

### 3. Dialogue Practice (15 min)
- Read Dialogue 2 together (teacher = Marina, student = Cleci)
- Then swap roles
- Then personalize: "What do YOU do in the morning / afternoon / at night?"
- Help her build 3 real sentences about her own day:
  - "I wake up at ___"
  - "I ___ in the morning"
  - "At night I ___"

### 4. Speaking Practice — Her Real Day (15 min)
- Ask: "Tell me about your day. Start from the morning."
- Let her speak freely (even broken English!) — note errors but don't interrupt
- After she finishes, help fix 2-3 key errors together
- Build a "My Day" mini-paragraph together:
  - "I wake up at seven. In the morning I ride my bike. In the afternoon I cook lunch. At night I watch Netflix."
- Have her repeat the paragraph 2-3 times

### 5. Listening Activity (5 min)
- Play the BBC video (or part of it) from the class page
- Ask: "What words did you hear?" (any word counts!)
- Celebrate any recognition — "wake up", "morning", "night" etc.

### 6. Self-Study Tool + Next Week (10 min)
- Demo Google Translate voice feature (share screen)
- Together, try saying 3 words into the mic: "wake up", "cook", "every day"
- Celebrate when Google understands her!
- Preview next week: "Next class we talk about Netflix and things you love and hate!"

## Notes for After Class
- [ ] Can she produce "I + verb + time phrase" unprompted?
- [ ] Which time phrases does she mix up? (morning/afternoon/night)
- [ ] Did she try the Google Translate mic tool?
- [ ] Which vocabulary words need more repetition next week?
- [ ] Was the pacing right? (too fast / too slow / just right)

## Key Phrases to Drill
These should become automatic (add to ongoing list):
- "I wake up at ___"
- "In the morning / In the afternoon / At night"
- "I cook / I ride my bike / I watch Netflix"
- "What do you do every day?"
- "Every day I ___"
