# Class 4 — Let's go to the supermarket! 🛒
## Teacher's Guide / Lesson Plan

### Objectives
By the end of this class, the student can:
- Express needs and wants ("I need milk", "I want chicken")
- Ask about prices ("How much is this?")
- React to prices ("It's expensive!", "It's cheap")
- Name 5 basic grocery items (milk, rice, chicken, water + review bread/fruit/coffee)
- Combine need/want with food vocabulary in practical sentences

### Class Structure (60 minutes)

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 0-5 min | Warm-up review | Quick Class 3 review: "Do you like Netflix? What show do you love?" |
| 5-15 min | Vocabulary | Go through 9 new words. Focus on "I need" vs "I want" — explain with PT (preciso vs quero). |
| 15-20 min | Pronunciation drill | "How much is this?" — she needs to say it fluently. Repeat 5+ times. |
| 20-30 min | Dialogue practice — 1 & 2 | Role-play. She is Cleci, you are Nelson. Then switch. |
| 30-40 min | Speaking: Real shopping list | Ask: "What do you need from the supermarket this week?" She makes a real list using "I need..." |
| 40-50 min | Dialogue practice — 3 & 4 | Focus on Dialogue 4 (comedy of forgetting coffee). Ask: "Do you forget things at the supermarket?" |
| 50-55 min | Duolingo setup | Help her install Duolingo on her phone LIVE. Set language, set 5-min goal, enable reminders. |
| 55-60 min | Wrap-up | Review: "I need ___", "I want ___", "How much is this?" Encouragement. |

### Speaking Practice Ideas
- Real shopping list: "What do you need?" → "I need rice, I need milk..."
- Price game: Say a price (high/low), she says "Expensive!" or "Cheap!"
- "Do you want chicken or fish?" → she practices "I want..."
- Compare: "I need coffee every day! Me too!" — connect to her real life
- Ask: "Is the supermarket expensive?" → real conversation about her local market

### Common Errors to Watch
- "I need of" (transfer from "preciso DE" in Portuguese) — should be just "I need milk" (no preposition)
- "How much is?" without "this/it" — drill the full phrase
- Pronouncing "chicken" as "shicken" — common for PT speakers
- "Expensive" pronunciation — the "x" is /ks/ not /sh/

### Self-Study Tool: Duolingo
- Install TOGETHER during class (no tech barriers)
- Set to MINIMUM goal (5 minutes/day)
- Connect to coffee routine: "When you make coffee, open Duolingo"
- Focus on Food and Shopping units
- Emphasize: it's a GAME, not homework. Missing a day = no guilt.

### Key Phrases to Drill
- "I need milk."
- "I want chicken."
- "How much is this?"
- "It's expensive!"
- "It's cheap."
- "Let's go!"

### Carry-Forward Vocabulary (use in ALL future classes)
- I need, I want, how much, expensive, cheap, milk, rice, chicken, water
- These ALL reappear in Class 5 (restaurant context)
- Core pattern: "I [need/want] + [food item]"
- Price question: "How much is [this/the item]?"
