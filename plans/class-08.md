# Class 8 — Clean the house, then relax! 🧹
## Teacher's Guide / Lesson Plan

### Objectives
By the end of this class, the student can:
- Name common household chores (clean, wash the dishes, sweep, make the bed)
- Use sequencing words to describe order (first, then, after that, finally)
- Give and understand simple instructions ("Clean the kitchen, please")
- Describe what she does after chores ("I relax", "I watch TV")
- Say "I'm done!" after completing something
- Combine chores with time/frequency ("I always clean on Saturday")

### Before Class (send Monday)

> 1. Listen to ALL vocabulary — focus on "First... Then... Finally..."
> 2. Read Dialogues 1 and 2 — notice the ORDER words

> See you in class! 🛋️

### Class Structure (60 minutes)

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 0-5 min | Warm-up review | "Describe one person in your family" (Class 7 review). Then: "What do you do on Saturday morning?" |
| 5-15 min | Vocabulary | 9 new words. Focus on "first/then/after that/finally" — these are the stars of this class. |
| 15-20 min | Sequencing drill | You say chores in random order, she puts them in sequence using "First... Then... After that... Finally..." |
| 20-30 min | Dialogue practice — 1 & 2 | Role-play: she is Cleci giving Nelson instructions. Encourage bossy tone — it's fun! |
| 30-40 min | Speaking: HER Saturday routine | She describes her actual Saturday chore sequence. You ask follow-ups: "And after that?" "What's next?" |
| 40-50 min | Dialogue practice — 3 & 4 | Focus on "I'm done!" and relaxation. Ask: "What do you do to relax?" |
| 50-55 min | Phone language change | Together, change her phone language to English. Walk through the settings. Reassure her she can change it back! |
| 55-60 min | Wrap-up | Full Saturday sequence: "First I ___, then I ___, after that ___, finally I relax!" Celebration! |

### Speaking Practice Ideas
- "Boss mode": she gives YOU instructions (clean this! wash that!) — practice imperatives
- Sequence challenge: describe making coffee step by step (first/then/finally)
- "What a mess!" game: describe a messy room, she says what to clean
- Her real routine: she narrates her Saturday morning in sequence
- Compare: "I always cook. Nelson never cooks!" — recycle Class 7 adverbs

### Common Errors to Watch
- "I make the clean" → "I clean" (Portuguese "fazer a limpeza" doesn't translate directly)
- "Wash the clothes" instead of "do the laundry" — both understood, but drill the chunk
- Sequencing word placement: "I first clean" → "First, I clean" (front position)
- "I'm finish" → "I'm done!" or "I finished!" — drill as chunk
- Pronunciation of "sweep" — /swiːp/ not "es-wee-pee"

### Self-Study Tool: Changing Phone Language to English
- Do this TOGETHER in class so she's not scared
- Go to Settings → Language → English (show her where it is)
- Reassure: "You can change it back anytime!"
- Why it works: she touches her phone 50+ times/day — passive English exposure
- Start with phone; next week try Netflix menus in English
- Key phrases she'll see: Settings, Messages, Camera, Photos, Calendar

### Key Phrases to Drill
- "First I clean, then I cook."
- "After that, I wash the dishes."
- "Finally, I relax!"
- "I'm done!"
- "What a mess!"
- "Clean the kitchen, please."
- "Let me help!"

### Carry-Forward Vocabulary (use in ALL future classes)
- Chores: clean, wash the dishes, sweep, make the bed
- Sequencing: first, then, after that, finally
- "I'm done!" works in any context (finishing food, finishing exercise, etc.)
- Sequencing words are essential for travel stories (Class 9)

### Progress Check (after Class 8)
Student should now be able to:
- ✅ Everything from Classes 1-7
- ✅ Name household chores
- ✅ Describe actions in sequence (first/then/after that/finally)
- ✅ Give simple instructions ("Clean the kitchen, please")
- ✅ Say "I'm done!" and describe relaxation activities
- ✅ Navigate phone in English (basic menus)
