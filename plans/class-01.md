# Class 1 — Good Morning! (Lesson Plan)

## Objectives
- Student can greet someone in English (Good morning / How are you?)
- Student can name 5+ breakfast items in English
- Student can say "I like..." and "I don't like..." about food
- Student learns about Anki as a self-study tool

## Before Class (send Monday)
- Share link to Class 1 page on the app
- Message: "Oi! Esta semana vamos comecar com o tema cafe da manha. No link voce vai encontrar vocabulario, um dialogo curto e exercicios. Tente fazer tudo antes da aula. Nao precisa acertar tudo — o importante e tentar! Qualquer duvida, me manda mensagem."

## Class Structure (60 min)

### 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Greet her in English: "Good morning! How are you?"
- Teach her to respond: "I'm fine, thank you. And you?"
- Practice this exchange 3x (swap roles)

### 2. Vocabulary Review (10 min)
- Go through each word from the list
- Say each word, she repeats (focus on pronunciation)
- Tricky sounds: "bread" (br-), "juice" (dʒuːs), "breakfast" (brekfəst)
- Quick game: you say the PT word, she says EN (then swap)

### 3. Exercise Correction (10 min)
- Go through her answers together
- Don't just correct — ask "why did you choose this?"
- If she got something wrong, repeat the word 3x together
- Celebrate what she got right!

### 4. Dialogue Practice (15 min)
- Read the dialogue together (you = Ana, she = Maria)
- Then swap roles
- Then: modify the dialogue with her real preferences
  - "Do you like coffee?" → her real answer
  - "I like coffee with ___ and ___" → her real breakfast

### 5. Speaking Practice (10 min)
- Ask her to describe her breakfast in English (even if broken!)
- Help her build sentences: "Every morning I have ___"
- Write down sentences she builds (she can review later)
- Example target: "Good morning. I like coffee. I like bread with butter. Breakfast is delicious."

### 6. Self-Study Tool + Next Week (10 min)
- Show her Anki (share screen, download together if needed)
- Create 3-4 flashcards together live (so she learns how)
- Explain spaced repetition simply: "O app mostra mais vezes as palavras que voce erra. Assim voce memoriza melhor!"
- Preview next week: "Next week we talk about your whole day!"

## Notes for After Class
- [ ] How was her pronunciation?
- [ ] Which words did she struggle with?
- [ ] Did she do the exercises before class?
- [ ] Did she install Anki?
- [ ] Anything to adjust for next week?

## Key Phrases to Drill
These should become automatic by the end of the course (start now):
- "Good morning!"
- "How are you?" / "I'm fine, thank you."
- "I like ___" / "I don't like ___"
