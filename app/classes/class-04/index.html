Dialogue 1 / Dialogo 1

Saturday morning. Cleci and Nelson check what they need to buy for the week. / Sabado de manha. Cleci e Nelson verificam o que precisam comprar pra semana.

Cleci
We need milk and bread.
Precisamos de leite e pao.
Nelson
And rice. I need rice.
E arroz. Eu preciso de arroz.
Cleci
Do you want chicken?
Voce quer frango?
Nelson
Yeah! I love chicken.
Sim! Eu amo frango.
Cleci
I need fruit too. And coffee!
Preciso de fruta tambem. E cafe!
Nelson
Okay! Let's go!
Ta! Vamos!

🌍 Did you know? / Voce sabia?

In the US and UK, you bag your own groceries — there's no "embalador" like in Brazil! The cashier asks "Paper or plastic?" (for the bag type). Just say: "Plastic, please."

Nos EUA e no Reino Unido, voce mesmo embala suas compras — nao tem embalador como no Brasil! O caixa pergunta "Paper or plastic?" (tipo de sacola). So diga: "Plastic, please."

Dialogue 2 / Dialogo 2

Cleci and Nelson are at the supermarket looking at prices. / Cleci e Nelson estao no supermercado olhando os precos.

Cleci
How much is this chicken?
Quanto e esse frango?
Nelson
Oh! It's expensive!
Ah! E caro!
Cleci
Yeah, it's expensive. And the rice?
E, e caro. E o arroz?
Nelson
The rice is cheap. Good!
O arroz e barato. Bom!
Cleci
I need water too.
Preciso de agua tambem.
Nelson
Water is cheap. Let's go!
Agua e barata. Vamos!

🏷️ Fun Fact / Curiosidade

In English, "cheap" for products is fine — "This rice is cheap!" But calling a PERSON "cheap" means "pao-duro" / "mao-de-vaca" (stingy)! So be careful: "The chicken is cheap" = good. "Nelson is cheap" = he doesn't want to spend money! 😄

Em ingles, "cheap" pra produtos e normal — "This rice is cheap!" Mas chamar uma PESSOA de "cheap" significa "pao-duro" / "mao-de-vaca"! Entao cuidado: "The chicken is cheap" = bom. "Nelson is cheap" = ele nao quer gastar dinheiro! 😄

Dialogue 3 / Dialogo 3

Cleci calls Marina after shopping to complain about prices. / Cleci liga pra Marina depois das compras pra reclamar dos precos.

Cleci
Marina! The chicken is so expensive!
Marina! O frango e tao caro!
Marina
I know! How much is it?
Eu sei! Quanto e?
Cleci
Too expensive! I don't like this supermarket.
Muito caro! Eu nao gosto desse supermercado.
Marina
I want cheap chicken!
Eu quero frango barato!
Cleci
Me too! And the milk is expensive too.
Eu tambem! E o leite e caro tambem.
Marina
Oh no! I need milk every day!
Ah nao! Eu preciso de leite todo dia!

Dialogue 4 / Dialogo 4

Back home. They unpack the groceries and realize they forgot something important! / De volta em casa. Eles guardam as compras e percebem que esqueceram algo importante!

Nelson
I have rice, bread, and fruit.
Eu tenho arroz, pao e fruta.
Cleci
Good! And chicken and milk.
Bom! E frango e leite.
Nelson
But I want coffee! We need coffee!
Mas eu quero cafe! Precisamos de cafe!
Cleci
Oh no! I forgot the coffee!
Ah nao! Esqueci o cafe!
Nelson
I need coffee in the morning!
Eu preciso de cafe de manha!
Cleci
Okay, okay! I go to the supermarket!
Ta bom, ta bom! Eu vou ao supermercado!
